These fonts are self-hosted so the app's "reading font" preference works
offline and without any third-party requests. Both are freely licensed.

## Atkinson Hyperlegible
- Files: `AtkinsonHyperlegible-{Regular,Bold,Italic,BoldItalic}.woff2`
- Designed by the Braille Institute of America.
- License: SIL Open Font License 1.1 (OFL).
- Source: Google Fonts (https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Atkinson+Hyperlegible)
  — the `latin` subset is bundled (covers English + Italian accented letters).
